# Mela Sales – short user guide

**Sign in** with Mela Auth (or email/password). Each business only ever sees its own data.

**Roles** – *Owner*: everything (products, prices, staff, reports, voiding, invoices of all cashiers). *Cashier*: record sales, add customers, take payments, print/share invoices of own sales.

**Record a sale** – pick products (or type a custom item), set quantity and an optional discount. Tax is added automatically from the product's tax rate. Choose:
- *Cash* – paid in full now.
- *Credit* – pick the customer; optionally enter what they paid now and a due date. The rest is added to their balance.

**Customer payments** – open the customer, tap *Add payment*. Part-payments are fine; the money is applied to the oldest unpaid invoices first. You cannot take more than the customer owes.

**Invoices** – every sale gets a number (e.g. `INV-2026-000012`). Open the PDF to print, or *Share* to get a link valid for 14 days and quick buttons for Telegram, WhatsApp and email. You can cancel the link any time.

**Mistakes** – sales cannot be edited or deleted. An owner can *Void* a sale with a reason; it stays on record (greyed out, excluded from totals), stock is returned, and the audit log shows who did it. Then enter the correct sale again.

**Recurring** – for subscriptions or installments, an owner sets customer, items, weekly/monthly and start date. Mela Sales creates the invoice automatically on each date; the customer pays it like any credit invoice.

**Reports** – daily, weekly or monthly totals with cash vs credit, plus an overdue list and CSV export.

**Language** – switch between English and አማርኛ in settings.
